Business rules retained from source: 100 coins per completed focus minute, 2500 coins per virtual tree, canceled sessions earn zero. New social request semantics and ranking opt-in are conservative completion choices for previously nonfunctional screens. No real planting, payments, email delivery, password reset, or file-upload services are configured. Legacy challenges lack eligibility metadata; they must be explicitly reviewed before reward redemption is enabled.

Follow-up rules approved by the owner: daily login awards 10 coins once per UTC day, with atomic wallet/ledger/challenge writes. Additional candidate milestones remain disabled. The nursery retains a monthly calendar: one plant per date with completed sessions, a trunk on interrupted-only dates, and soil on empty dates. Direct leaderboard opt-in and refresh make previously private progress discoverable without changing privacy defaults.

Strict focus adds an optional extension-backed flow: configured origin + immutable allowlist → monitor capability → allowed-tab check-ins → verified completion or interruption → wallet/calendar update. Normal focus remains the default. The server expires monitoring gaps at 90 seconds and forbids late completion rewards. Installation and physical sleep limits are documented in [STRICT_FOCUS.md](STRICT_FOCUS.md).
